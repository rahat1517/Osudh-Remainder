# Terms of Use

**Oshudh Reminder** · Draft for legal review

1. **What the service is.** The app reads prescriptions with AI to help you enter medicines, and
   reminds you when doses are due. It is a reminder tool, not a medical device, and gives no
   medical advice.
2. **You confirm every medicine.** The AI can misread prescriptions. You are responsible for checking
   every value against the prescription before confirming. Never change a dose without your doctor.
3. **Reminders can fail.** Phones can block or delay alarms (battery savers, permissions, switched-off
   devices). Do not rely on the app as the only way to remember critical medicines.
4. **Emergencies.** The app is not for emergencies. Call 999 or go to the nearest hospital.
5. **Family access.** You decide who can see your medicines by sharing invite codes, and you can remove
   access at any time. Caregivers must use access only to help the patient.
6. **Your account.** Keep your phone number secure. You can delete your account at any time.
7. **Acceptable use.** Do not upload images that are not prescriptions or that contain other people's
   data without their consent, and do not attempt to access others' data.
8. **Changes and termination.** We may change these terms with notice in the app, and may suspend
   accounts that abuse the service.
9. **Liability.** To the extent permitted by law, the service is provided "as is", without warranties.
10. **Law.** These terms are governed by the laws of Bangladesh.
